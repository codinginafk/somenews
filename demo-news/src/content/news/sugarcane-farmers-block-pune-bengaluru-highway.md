**Short answer:** Sugarcane growers in Karnataka blocked National Highway 48 (Pune-Bengaluru highway) at Hirebagewadi and Hattargi toll plazas in Belagavi district. Farmers demand raising sugarcane Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) to ₹5,500 per tonne, revising sugar Minimum Support Price (MSP) to ₹60 per kg, and the resignation of Union Food Minister Pralhad Joshi. Police have diverted commercial freight and non-emergency traffic through detour routes.

## 1. What the highway blockade looks like

Protesting farmers parked tractor-trolleys loaded with harvested cane, alongside bullock carts, across the highway lanes beneath overhead "Pune / Bengaluru" signboards. Demonstrators staged a sit-in across both carriage directions.

Several farmers held brooms and whips, while others performed "Urulu Seve" by rolling on the asphalt. Protest organizers set up roadside tents near the toll plazas serving drinking water, tea, and rotti-bhaji to thousands arriving from regional talukas.

Heavy freight vehicles have been stopped completely in long queues extending several kilometres. Police and organizers allowed emergency passenger vehicles and two-wheelers through service lanes, while diverting the majority of vehicles onto state roads.

## 2. Core demands: prices, profits, and political accountability

The protest reflects worsening financial strain across sugarcane belts in Karnataka and western Maharashtra. Farmers state that sharp increases in diesel, fertilizer, cutting, and transport costs have outpaced factory procurement rates.

Their key charter of demands includes:

- **Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP):** Raise raw sugarcane purchase price to ₹5,500 per tonne.
- **Minimum Support Price (MSP) for Sugar:** Revise the floor price of processed sugar from current benchmarks to ₹60 per kg.
- **By-product Profit Sharing:** Enact legislation requiring cooperative, public, and private sugar mills to treat farmers as shareholders, distributing profits made from molasses, ethanol, co-generated power, oxygen, bio-fertilizer, and press mud.
- **Weighing Transparency:** Install government-supervised computerized weighing scales and transparent recovery assessment systems at all mill gates.
- **Ethanol Procurement:** Increase central state procurement of fuel ethanol produced in Karnataka from sugarcane juice and maize.
- **Market Dual-Pricing:** Establish separate sugar rate tiers for domestic households and commercial bulk consumers.
- **Harvesting Calendar:** Enforce a strict district-administered cane cutting and transport schedule.

Protest leaders also called for the immediate resignation of Union Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution Minister Pralhad Joshi. Farm union leaders said the minister invited their delegation to New Delhi for consultations but failed to hear their grievances, which farmers termed an insult to agrarian representatives.

## 3. Alternative routes for commuters and logistics

The Maharashtra Highway Police and Karnataka local authorities have established three diversion corridors to navigate around the blocked stretches at Kognoli, Hattargi, and Hirebagewadi toll plazas.

| Route Type | Alignment | Best Suited For |
|---|---|---|
| Route 1 (Northern bypass) | Satara -> Rahimatpur -> Pusesawali -> Nevri -> Vita -> Tasgaon -> Miraj -> Kagwad -> Raibag -> Gokak -> Belagavi | Light passenger vehicles |
| Route 2 (Southern bypass) | Satara -> Karad -> Peth Naka -> Islampur -> Ashta -> Miraj -> Kagwad -> Raibag -> Gokak -> Belagavi | Local intercity traffic |
| Long-Distance Corridor | Pune -> Solapur (NH-65) -> Vijayapura (NH-52) -> Chitradurga (NH-50) -> Bengaluru (NH-48) | Heavy commercial freight |

Authorities contacted Google Maps navigation services to designate the affected toll gates as temporarily closed to prevent drivers from entering the bottleneck. Travelers are urged to postpone non-essential transit across the Kolhapur-Belagavi border zone until clearance is confirmed.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Is the Pune-Bengaluru highway fully closed to all vehicles?**
A: Main carriage lanes at Hattargi and Hirebagewadi remain blocked by tractors. Emergency vehicles and two-wheelers use service roads, while intercity traffic is diverted onto state highways.

**Q: What is the recommended bypass route for trucks moving between Mumbai and Bengaluru?**
A: Long-distance freight should take the Solapur-Vijayapura-Chitradurga corridor (NH-65 to NH-52 to NH-50) to bypass Kolhapur and Belagavi entirely.

**Q: What is the current FRP for sugarcane versus the demanded rate?**
A: The central statutory Fair and Remunerative Price sits near ₹3,400 per tonne depending on base sucrose recovery, while farmers demand ₹5,500 per tonne to offset rising input costs.
