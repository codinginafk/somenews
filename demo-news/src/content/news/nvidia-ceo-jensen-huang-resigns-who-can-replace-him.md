**Short answer:** There was no heir waiting. Nvidia's board was four days into an independent investigation into reports that founder and CEO Jensen Huang had been the target of a foreign intelligence effort when he resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer and a director, effective immediately on November 12, 2026. The succession Nvidia announced is a placeholder: Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress as interim chief executive while the board searches for a permanent replacement, considering internal and external candidates, with Harvey C. Jones appointed the company's first-ever board chair. The shares did not shrug. They fell more than 13% at the open and were still down about 5% on the morning of the announcement.

## 1. The four days that produced the resignation

Nvidia's own newsroom publishes the sequence, and it is the most complete record of what happened.

On November 8, the board issued a statement saying it was initiating an independent investigation in light of recent reporting that founder and CEO Jensen Huang had been the target of a foreign intelligence effort. A committee of independent directors, assisted by outside independent counsel, would oversee it. The statement said the board had no reason to believe that either national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised, that the inquiry would examine all available information, that Huang fully supported the review and had committed to cooperating, and that the board had confidence in Huang and his leadership team.

Two days later, on November 10, the independent members of the board announced that they had retained Joon H. Kim, the former acting United States Attorney for the Southern District of New York, and his firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to conduct a thorough and independent investigation.

Then on November 12 came the CEO transition.

CNN described the sequence as a quick reversal by the Nvidia board, which initially said it had confidence in Huang in its Sunday statement and no reason to believe that national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised. ABC News reported that Huang admitted in an internal message to Nvidia staff that he had been unfaithful to his wife, and that the board had opened the inquiry after reporting that he was in a close relationship with Alice Huang, a Chinese citizen who has worked for the Singapore-based technology company and Nvidia customer Megaspeed, whom United States officials suspected of working with Chinese intelligence. ABC News noted that it could not verify that reporting or an alleged video, that the Chinese government called the allegations baseless and purely slanderous smears against China, and that neither Alice Huang nor Megaspeed International responded to its request for comment.

## 2. What Nvidia announced

The company's release is precise about what changed and what did not.

Jensen Huang resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer and a member of the Board, effective immediately. Colette Kress, who has been Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer since joining Nvidia in September 2013 and who spent nearly 25 years in finance roles at leading technology companies including Cisco and Microsoft, has agreed to serve as Interim Chief Executive Officer. She continues as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer while serving as interim chief until a permanent successor has been named. The board is initiating a search and will consider internal and external candidates.

Harvey C. Jones, an entrepreneur, technology executive and active venture investor of more than 30 years who served as Chief Executive Officer and Executive Chairman of Synopsys, was appointed the first-ever Chair of the Board.

Jones's statement is the part worth reading closely. "Colette has helped lead NVIDIA through a period of extraordinary growth and has a deep understanding of our business, customers, operations and financial position," he said. "The Board has full confidence in Colette and the leadership team, and we expect the company's operations, customer commitments and product roadmap to continue without interruption."

Kress's own statement pointed at the founder: "NVIDIA's strength has always come from its people, and together with our leadership team, I am committed to building on the extraordinary foundation Jensen created over the last 33 years."

The release also confirmed that Nvidia would report its third-quarter fiscal 2027 results on Tuesday, November 17, at approximately 1.20 pm Pacific Time, with the earnings call at 2 pm Pacific.

## 3. What the market did

The claim that a succession would be orderly for investors did not survive the tape.

CNN reported that Nvidia shares dropped more than 10% in pre-market trading on Thursday. ABC News reported that shares tumbled about 5% as of Thursday morning, recovering some of the losses from a plunge of more than 13% at the open.

The scale of what was being repriced is easy to miss. CNN noted that Nvidia became the world's first trillion-dollar company last year, and that the chips Huang's company built in 1993 became critical to the AI infrastructure, putting Nvidia at the centre of the AI buildout. A company whose founder left the same week its board was investigating a foreign-intelligence report about him is a different risk instrument from the one that crossed a trillion.

This is the pattern we have tracked across the [Nvidia hardware cargo heist](/nvidia-gpu-cargo-heist-data-center-chips-stolen/) and the [FBI confirmation of the federal inquiry](/trump-confirms-fbi-investigating-jensen-huang-nvidia/): the physical and regulatory exposure around this company has been widening all autumn, and the leadership change lands in the middle of it.

## 4. What the record does not settle

The permanent chief executive is unknown. The board said it would consider internal and external candidates and would report back, and no candidate list has been published.

The investigation's findings are unknown. Nvidia's statements describe a process, not an outcome, and no conclusion has been released.

And the facts of the underlying reporting remain contested. ABC News could not verify the reporting or the alleged video, the Chinese government denied the allegations, and the party at the centre of them did not respond to ABC News' request for comment. Our [explainer on the federal probe](/trump-confirms-fbi-investigating-jensen-huang-nvidia/) sets out what each side has actually said.

## 5. The verdict: false, with the context that a bench was found

The claim fails. No internal heir was named, and Nvidia's own release says the opposite: the board is opening a search that includes external candidates, and the interim chief is the chief financial officer who also keeps the finance job.

The context is that this was never a planned transition dressed up as a succession. The board's own timeline puts an independent investigation with outside counsel four days ahead of the resignation, and the market read it that way, with a 13% opening fall. What Nvidia has is a caretaker with the board's confidence and a first-ever independent chair, and the company's next results on November 17.
