**Short answer:** It is accurate that an unprecedented nationwide crackdown is underway ahead of the October 10 "Chalo Delhi" mega-protest. The Election Commission's Special Intensive Revision did result in over 130 million voter deletions, and authorities are actively detaining thousands of activists, though the exact figure of 10,000 remains a partisan estimate.

## 1. The 130 million deleted voters trigger

The primary catalyst for the "Chalo Delhi" mega-protest (following the [October 2 CJP protests](/cjp-protest-october-2/)) is the Special Intensive Revision of voter lists overseen by Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. The CJP and united opposition groups allege this resulted in the deletion of over 130 million names nationwide.

The government maintains this is a routine purification of duplicate entries. However, the opposition claims it is a targeted strike on their voter base. The sheer scale of the deletions has fueled intense political mobilization, particularly among Gen Z voters who feel their fundamental rights are being erased.

## 2. Mass detentions and the nationwide crackdown

Ahead of the planned Saturday march to Delhi's Jantar Mantar, state police forces have initiated severe preventative measures. CJP spokesperson Ashutosh Ranka announced that an estimated 10,000 volunteers, students, and political activists have been detained across Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, and Rajasthan.

While the exact number of 10,000 is difficult to independently verify, widespread reports confirm a massive coordinated effort to prevent protesters from reaching the capital. Reports backed by video evidence show students traveling by train from Pune to Delhi being intercepted in Madhya Pradesh and loaded into police buses. Key figures like CJP co-in-charge Deepak Baliyan and SFI's Saharanpur convener Arif have also been detained. Left-wing groups allege police are conducting midnight visits to activists' homes to intimidate families.

## 3. Political theater and the barricaded capital

The crackdown extends beyond grassroots activists. Earlier this week, top Congress leaders Rahul Gandhi and Priyanka Gandhi Vadra were briefly detained by Delhi Police while attempting to reach Jantar Mantar. The ruling BJP dismissed the outrage, with spokesperson Gaurav Bhatia characterizing the protesters as an irresponsible and anarchist opposition, claiming their allegations of voter suppression lack evidence.

Protests have already erupted in the South. In Bengaluru, hundreds of CJP supporters marched with placards reading "Stop the Murder of Democracy," highlighting the widespread frustration among youth demographics.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Why is the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) protesting?**
A: The CJP, a youth-led movement, is demanding the immediate resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar over the deletion of 130 million names from the national voter lists.

**Q: Has the October 10 protest been canceled due to the detentions?**
A: No. Despite heavy barricading in Delhi and thousands of alleged detentions, the "Chalo Delhi" mega-protest is still scheduled, setting the stage for a major democratic showdown.
