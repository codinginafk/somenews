**Short answer:** Early on September 27, massive protests broke out at Lovely Professional University following claims that a student was raped and had died by suicide. While university officials initially labeled the claims as baseless rumours, police later registered an FIR based on student statements, though details remain highly contested.

## 1. What the viral post shows

According to protesting students, a female student was assaulted on campus roughly three days before the unrest occurred at the G-3 hostel. The claim suggests that instead of launching a formal investigation, the university sent the student home, and her roommate later informed others that the student had died by suicide. This narrative fueled intense frustration, leading to roughly 2,000 students gathering around the hostel, smashing windows, and blocking the Delhi-Amritsar highway. Students argued that the administration was slow to respond and unwilling to be transparent about safety issues, sparking an open revolt.

## 2. What the data actually says

The official response initially painted a completely different picture. LPU registrar Dr. Monica Gulati called the claims false and fabricated, urging the public not to share misinformation. SSP Kapurthala Gaurav Toora stated that his office received no complaint regarding the case prior to the campus chaos. He suggested that the outrage may have been conflated with an older, unrelated video from August 11 involving a suicide at a different department, similar to misinformation that previously spread at Chitkara University.

However, as the day progressed, DIG (Jalandhar Range) Naveen Singla indicated that preliminary investigations supported the students' timeline that an incident had occurred about three days earlier. An FIR was formally registered based on recorded statements, and a Special Investigation Team was announced to gather digital and technical evidence.

## 3. What's missing / the footnote

The situation is currently developing, and the conflicting statements from different police officials highlight the confusion surrounding the case. It remains unclear what specific charges the FIR contains, whether the property damage charges will be levied against the students or external individuals, and how the administration will address the broader demands for all-female staffing in the girls' hostels.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Did the protests exclusively involve LPU students?**
A: Students claim the protests were initially peaceful, but non-students entered when the gates opened and escalated the vandalism. Police are currently assessing property damage to determine legal action.

**Q: Has the university admitted to any cover-up?**
A: No. The university administration maintains that the claims of a cover-up are fabricated, though student union representatives have now been invited to take part in the ongoing probe.
