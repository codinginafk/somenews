**Short answer:** A protest led by Dhruv Rathee and the CJP is scheduled for October 10 at Jantar Mantar. However, claims that Amit Shah deployed 22,000 CRPF personnel to suppress it are unsubstantiated. Verified reporting shows no such extraordinary troop mobilization.

## 1. What the viral post shows

A political protest recently took place at Freedom Park in Bengaluru. YouTuber Dhruv Rathee and actor Prakash Raj demanded the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. Following this event, a dramatic claim began circulating online.

The text accompanying the upcoming October 10 National Protest by the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) states: *"A country uses more than 22,000 Central Armed Police Force (CAPF) and CRPF to guard a small part of city block of its national capital... What the government is aiming to do is suppress any possible public assembly even before it takes place."* It directly implicates Home Minister Amit Shah in utilizing "brute strength" to silence the anticipated crowds. This mirrors tactics seen in other exaggerated claims, such as those regarding the [recent bank strike deferment](/bank-strike-deferred-7-day-work-week/) or [UK crime statistics](/britain-crime-claim-vs-data/).

## 2. What the data actually says

The core of the viral claim—the deployment of 22,000 CRPF personnel—is not supported by public records or media coverage of the event preparations. A review of verified reporting leading up to the October 10 rally reveals no deployment orders of this magnitude. 

Jantar Mantar regularly sees police presence ahead of organized protests. A deployment of 22,000 personnel is vastly different. It would squeeze a massive fraction of the entire CRPF into one small street. Consider historical comparisons from the large-scale farmers' protests. Deployments at specific border points numbered in the lower thousands. They never reached tens of thousands at a small site like Jantar Mantar.

## 3. What's missing / the footnote

The figure of 22,000 appears completely fabricated. Its likely purpose is to amplify the perceived threat level ahead of the upcoming "Chappal Protest". Student groups like AISA and SFI are coordinating a significant gathering with political figures. However, the state's security response has not been documented at the scale claimed in these messages.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Is Dhruv Rathee organizing a protest at Jantar Mantar on October 10?**
A: Yes. Following the Bengaluru demonstration, Rathee urged supporters to attend a follow-up protest organized by the CJP at Jantar Mantar on October 10, 2026.

**Q: Are there CAPF personnel deployed at Jantar Mantar?**
A: Standard security protocols for large scheduled protests do involve Delhi Police and sometimes CAPF units for crowd control. There is no evidence supporting the figure of 22,000 personnel.
